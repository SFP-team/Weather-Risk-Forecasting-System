4. When the winter calendar is the main result

All three must hold, otherwise the managed-cycle scan (section 8) is the main result: |lat| ≥ 23.44; class known and not Evergreen; at least 12 of 15 winters reach harvest (P:1205). The chill requirement itself applies, and chill shortfall is counted, when the site is outside the tropics and its class is known and not Evergreen.

8. Managed-cycle scan (evergreen and tropical sites)

  • Budbreak is forced on the 1st and 15th of every month (24 starts) in each year 2011 to 2025; bloom and harvest follow the same GDD clock (P:1026, P:1088).
  • Each start-year is complete; stalled slow (bloom longer than 66 days, or first harvest more than 150 days after bloom start); stalled at the 300-day limit; or weather incomplete, which is left out (P:1030).
  • Crop-loss families: bud freeze, flowering freeze, fruit frost, cycle failure (stalled ÷ (complete + stalled)). A start where any recurs in at least 50% of cycles is not favourable; a start needs 12 cycles (P:1040).
  • Other weather is a share of stage days: heat days ÷ fruit days, heavy-rain days ÷ harvest days, moderate infection days ÷ bloom-start-to-harvest-end days, no-flight days ÷ bloom days (P:1043).
  • Favourable starts = those no other start beats on every measure, plus starts within one affected cycle or one affected day per cycle of them (P:1077).

10. Year-round planner Test branch only

On branch evergreen-analyses and the test copy at port 8790; not on main or the live app.

  • Weekly hazards: week = min(⌊(day of year − 1)/7⌋, 51); per week, years with at least one frost (Tmin ≤ 0), hard freeze (≤ −2.2), hot (Tmax ≥ 32), severe heat (≥ 35), heavy rain (≥ 10 mm) or disease day (section 5 proxy). A week counts only if every day has data. hazard_calendar() in production.py.
  • El Niño / La Niña month: NOAA ONI ≥ +0.5 / ≤ −0.5 for at least 5 overlapping 3-month seasons in a row, stored at the centre month. scripts/build_enso.py.
  • Warm year: month mean Tmean minus that month's 2011 to 2025 average. Market planner: starts ranked by no recurring crop loss, then the share of years whose first pick falls in the chosen months. dist/yearround.js.
