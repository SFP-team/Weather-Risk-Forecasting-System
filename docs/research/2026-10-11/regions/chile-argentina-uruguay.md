# Chile, Argentina and Uruguay: source inventory for the blueberry weather-risk app

Slug: `chile-argentina-uruguay`. Checked on 2026-10-11. Companion file: `chile-argentina-uruguay.sources.json` (64 sources, 16 jobs).

Method note: I covered Chile directly. A delegated sub-pass, run on the same date under the same rules, covered Argentina and Uruguay; I spot-checked four of its sources (ABC reports page, Res. SGA 77/2019, EEAOC agromet, INIA Salto Grande catalogue) and they matched. Station samples were small single requests: ten RAN requests of 15 days each for the 2022 frost, one EEAOC frost query, two NASA POWER point calls and a few open-data API queries. Nothing was stored in the repository. My own reasoning is marked [inference].

## 1. Scope and summary

Scope: Chile (Coquimbo to Los Lagos), Argentina (Tucumán, Entre Ríos, with notes on Corrientes and Buenos Aires) and Uruguay (Salto and the south). Categories: stations, phenology, orchard census, exports, damage and declarations, cultivar traits, and narrative reports for a reviewed evidence library.

Five most valuable findings:

1. **The "November 2022" Chilean frost was on the morning of 31 October 2022, and the app's check window missed it.** Open hourly RAN stations show -2.0 C at Coihueco, -2.2 C at Linares 2 (Longaví), -1.7 C at San Ignacio and -1.2 C at Santa Rosa (Chillán) on 31 October ([RAN Agromet](https://www.agromet.cl/datos-historicos)). The DMC October 2022 bulletin confirms -2.2 C at Linares on day 31 ([DMC bulletin](https://climatologia.meteochile.gob.cl/publicaciones/boletinAgroclimatico/boletinAgroclimatico202210.pdf)). The repository check used 1 to 30 November 2022 (`docs/research/2026-10-05/ibo_event_checks.json`), so it never looked at 31 October. NASA POWER cells near Linares and Chillán give +4.6 to +5.1 C that day, a warm error of about 6 to 7 C on a frost night. See section 6.1.
2. **Chile has an open, block-level orchard census.** The CIREN-ODEPA catastro on the ODEPA CKAN portal gives every blueberry block with commune, variety, planting year, irrigation method and area, 1999-2025, under CC BY ([datos.odepa.gob.cl](https://datos.odepa.gob.cl/dataset/catastro-fruticola)). The 2024 file has 621 blueberry blocks in Coihueco alone. A CIREN map service adds farm polygons with the top three species ([CIREN MapServer](https://esri.ciren.cl/server/rest/services/IDEMINAGRI/CATASTRO_FRUTICOLA/MapServer)).
3. **Harvest timing by region can be measured two ways.** ODEPA customs data give monthly fresh blueberry exports by region of origin from 1998 ([ODEPA comercio exterior](https://datos.odepa.gob.cl/dataset/comercio-exterior)). The Blueberry Committee weekly Crop Report gives weekly tonnes, weekly regional shares and tonnes by variety and region ([week 08 2026 PDF](https://frutasdechile.cl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/260219-Blueberries-Crop-Report-w08.pdf)).
4. **Argentina has a second measurable missed-frost case.** The EEAOC frost service returned 49 Tucumán stations for 10 to 16 July 2024: Monte Grande 2 (Famaillá, 397 m) reached -4.4 C with 41.9 h below 0 C; INTA SIGA Famaillá recorded -2.7 C on 11 and 14 July ([EEAOC agromet](https://agromet.eeaoc.gob.ar/), [INTA SIGA](https://siga.inta.gob.ar/)). INTA Concordia recorded -3.5 C on 9 and 12 July 2024 ([INTA report](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/concordia-efecto-de-las-heladas-en-producciones-agropecuarias)).
5. **Official monthly bulletins carry dated stage statements and frost-damage annexes.** INIA's regional agroclimatic-risk bulletins run monthly since at least September 2018 for every region. The Maule November 2022 issue says blueberries were at fruit set, with colour break in the Secano Interior ([INIA bulletins](http://riesgoclimatico.inia.cl/public/publicaciones)). Emergency resolutions cite these annexes, for example the Putaendo frost emergency of 28 October 2022 ([Diario Oficial CVE 2210847](https://www.diariooficial.interior.gob.cl/publicaciones/2022/11/04/43392/01/2210847.pdf)).

## 2. Production context in brief

**Chile.** The 2025-26 export season reached 92,000 t of fresh blueberries; Maule shipped 37%, Ñuble 26%, Biobío 12%, La Araucanía 8%, Los Ríos 8%, O'Higgins 3% and Los Lagos 2% ([Crop Report week 08](https://frutasdechile.cl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/260219-Blueberries-Crop-Report-w08.pdf)). Legacy (26,811 t) and Duke (18,413 t) led, followed by Brightwell, Blue Ribbon and Suziblue; "renewal" varieties were 24% of shipments (same source). Exports run from ISO week 35 to about week 15, with the bulk in weeks 48 to 4 (same source). The 2024 catastro file lists blueberry blocks in Coquimbo (133 rows), Maule (3,775), Ñuble (1,939), Biobío (882), Araucanía (1,045), Los Ríos (722) and Los Lagos (333) ([ODEPA catastro](https://datos.odepa.gob.cl/dataset/catastro-fruticola)). Northern highbush dominates the large regions, and low-chill plantings sit north of about Maule, often in pots and tunnels (IBO 2025, record `cl-chill-zones` in `dist/evidence/ibo-regional-v1.json`). Harvest runs about October-November in the north, late November to January in the south-central zone and late January to early March in the south ([Retamales et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1590/0100-2945-446/13), already cited in `docs/weather/GLOBAL_PHENOLOGY_REVIEW.md`). Frost control and insurance are uneven: an exporter said many small and medium Ñuble growers have neither ([Portal Frutícola](https://www.portalfruticola.com/noticias/2022/11/09/chilecomo-se-han-afectado-los-arandanos-de-nuble-por-las-heladas/)).

**Argentina.** Production is southern highbush in two belts: the Northwest (Tucumán piedmont, Salta, Catamarca) harvests about ISO weeks 33 to 47, and the Northeast (Concordia, Corrientes) weeks 37 to 49 ([MAGyP 2020](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/arandanos-oct-2020.pdf)). In 2017 Tucumán area was concentrated in Monteros (579 ha), Chicligasta (225 ha) and Famaillá (117 ha) ([INTA Famaillá 2019](https://repositorio.inta.gob.ar/xmlui/bitstream/handle/20.500.12123/4671/INTA_CRTucuman-Santiago_EEAFamailla_RiosdeGonzalez_L_Comercializacion_y_competitividad_del_arandano_argentino.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y), via the sub-pass). IBO reports 60% of orchards under structure in 2022/23 (record `ar-protected`), and the ABC secretary said about 90% of area has frost protection ([Federcitrus, Aug 2024](https://www.federcitrus.org/primera-proyeccion-de-la-temporada-de-arandanos-argentinos/)). Tafí del Valle (about 2,000 m) is a high-elevation northern highbush trial site ([Lobo Zavalía et al. 2018](https://www.scielo.org.ar/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S1851-30182018000200002)).

**Uruguay.** A small industry. INIA documents give harvest in the Litoral Norte (Salto) in October-November and in the south in November-December, with O'Neal dominant ([INIA ainfo](https://ainfo.inia.uy/digital/bitstream/item/12472/1/20-ARANDANO-VILARO.pdf)). OPYPA puts blueberries under 2% of fruit exports ([OPYPA Anuario 2024](https://descargas.mgap.gub.uy/OPYPA/Anuarios/Anuarioopypa2024/CP/13/CP13web/CP13-Fruticulturaycitricultura.pdf)). No current official area figure was found.

## 3. What the app already has for this scope

- **Benchmark sites** (`pipelines/weather/benchmark_sites.json`): cl-ovalle-coquimbo, cl-linares-maule, cl-chillan-nuble, cl-los-angeles-biobio, cl-osorno-los-lagos, ar-famailla-tucuman, ar-tafi-del-valle, ar-concordia-entre-rios. All Chilean bloom months are empty. Model harvest windows for south-central Chile are December-January against published November-January. Famaillá is modelled deciduous with bloom August-September against published June-August. Tafí del Valle bloom is modelled August-September against published October.
- **Sources already cited there:** Retamales 2014, ODEPA 2002, FAS GAIN CI2022-0030, the FreshPlaza.es copy of the committee's November 2022 release, Hirzel 2023, the UACh fertilisation manual, Mundoagro week-7 shares, SENASA January 2026 news, MAGyP 2020, Lobo Zavalía 2018, La Gaceta July 2024, EEAOC RA25 (403), Rivadeneira 2012 and 2022, Entre Ríos Ya 2026. The new inventory avoids repeating these except where a fresh check changed their status.
- **IBO records in scope** (`dist/evidence/ibo-regional-v1.json`): `cl-2022-11-frost-hail` (frost and hail between Linares and Chillán, November 2022), `cl-2022-12-heat` (December 2022 heat concentrated harvest), `cl-2025-26-early-ripening` (Duke about 20 days early), `cl-chill-zones`, `ar-protected`, `ar-harvest`. None carries a station value or a commune list. CAU-01 and CAU-07 add both.
- **Known gaps:** INIA, CIREN and SciELO PDFs returned 403 during compilation; planting months for Maule, Ñuble and Biobío were not found; Tucumán piedmont bloom months were inferred from a July 2024 frost report; the grid cells missed the Chilean 2022 frost (minimum +7.59 C at Linares and +7.66 C at Chillán in the 1 to 30 November window, `docs/research/2026-10-05/ibo_event_checks.json`).

## 6. How each source maps to model checks

### 6.1 Station evidence for the "November 2022" frost in Maule and Ñuble

**What the reports say.** The committee release of 16 November 2022 dates the frost to 1 November, "desde la región del Maule-Sur a la del Bío Bío", with most effect in Ñuble, "principalmente a la comuna de Coihueco y con menor intensidad en las comunas de San Ignacio y El Carmen, lo mismo que en Maule-Sur hacia la cordillera", damage of 10 to 20% and a loss near 900 t ([Frutas de Chile](https://frutasdechile.cl/news/minimos-efectos-de-las-heladas-lluvia-y-granizo-de-noviembre-sobre-la-produccion-de-arandanos-de-chile/)). An exporter in San Carlos says the frost came on 31 October or 1 November depending on the zone, mostly at fruit set, with damage from none to above 80% ([Portal Frutícola, 9 Nov 2022](https://www.portalfruticola.com/noticias/2022/11/09/chilecomo-se-han-afectado-los-arandanos-de-nuble-por-las-heladas/)). The vice president of the Ñuble farmers association dates it to 31 October, places damage east of Route 5 between Longaví and Cabrero, names Cato and Coihueco, and names Brightwell, Legacy and Duke ([Blueberries Consulting, 11 Nov 2022](https://blueberriesconsulting.com/en/chile-helada-provoca-perdidas-en-huertos-de-arandanos-en-nuble/)).

Relative humidity at the four sub-zero stations was 82 to 93% at the time of minimum, which points to a radiative frost [inference]. No station of the ten fell below 0 C on 1 November.

**What the grid shows.** NASA POWER daily T2M_MIN (one API call per cell): cell -36.0, -71.875 (Linares cell) 3.69 C on 30 October, 5.07 C on 31 October, 9.59 C on 1 November; cell -36.5, -71.875 (Chillán cell) 2.24, 4.56 and 10.08 C ([POWER API](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/point)). The repository check (`ibo_event_checks.json`) searched 1 to 30 November UTC and reported +7.59 and +7.66 C on 5 and 6 November.

**Reading.** [inference] The damaging night was 30 to 31 October 2022, with a weaker or local follow-up on 1 November at some sites; the industry date of 1 November is the report's date, not a measured one. The event is patchy: the coldest stations sit east of Route 5 and toward the foothills (Coihueco, Longaví), while the coast-ward and southern stations stayed above 0 C, matching the "hacia la cordillera" wording. The grid cell is warmer than the coldest station by 6.6 to 7.3 C on 31 October and never reaches 0 C, so the app would miss the event even with the right date. Recommended model checks: (a) widen IBO event windows by at least 3 days either side of the reported month; (b) use CAU-01 to set a cold-tail Tmin correction for clear, calm nights in October and November; (c) record stage as fruit set and late bloom (from the exporter and INIA Maule statements), so the right risk family is fruit_frost and late flowering_freeze, not bud freeze.

## 7. Access, licensing and language risks

- **Undocumented endpoints.** RAN (getGraphData.php), EEAOC (services/*.php) and INTA SIGA (obfuscated php path) all work but are undocumented. They can change, and heavy use may be unwelcome. Ask the operators (RAN: info@agromet.cl, listed on the site) before any backfill.
- **TLS problems.** agromet.cl fails strict certificate checks; www.agrometeorologia.cl has a name mismatch (bare host works); heladas.minagri.gob.cl returned a proxy SSL error. Pipelines need tolerant but logged TLS handling.
- **Login walls.** DMC data products and web services sit behind a user login (inferred from the login link). EEAOC shows auth and request routes. INIA DSpace returned 401 for at least one item.
- **Licences.** CC BY: ODEPA catastro and trade data, CR2MET (CC BY 4.0), argentina.gob.ar pages (CC BY 4.0). Uruguay catalogue: Licencia de DAG de Uruguay. US federal work: FAS GAIN. "All rights reserved": ABC reports. Not found: RAN, INIA bulletins, DMC, CIREN MapServer, Frutas de Chile, EEAOC, INTA SIGA. Treat "not found" as permission needed before public display of raw tables.
- **Personal data.** Catastro exploitation numbers and CIREN property roll numbers identify farms. Publish only aggregates.
- **Industry voice.** Committee and press numbers are estimates and sometimes misdate events (1 November versus 31 October 2022). Keep reported and measured dates as separate fields.
- **Language.** Almost everything is Spanish. Field names and stage terms (cuaja, pinta, plena flor, fruto cuajado) need a glossary in the extraction prompts. The Crop Report is in English.
- **Paywalls.** iQonsulting (commercial), Upefruy figures behind El Observador subscription.

## 8. Gaps: what I looked for and could not find

- **Chile bloom months by region for Biobío, Araucanía, Los Ríos and Los Lagos**: no dated, multi-year bloom observations. The INIA regional bulletins rarely mention blueberries (none in October 2024 Maule, Ñuble or Los Ríos). The UdeC Chillán phenology thesis (2009-10, O'Neal and Duke) has only its 2-page abstract online. The UACh manual figure remains an image.
- **Planting months** for Maule, Ñuble and Biobío: not found (the catastro gives planting year, not month).
- **A frost emergency declaration for Ñuble or Maule in October-November 2022**: not found. The closest is the Putaendo (Valparaíso) frost emergency of 28 October 2022.
- **Agroseguros claims by crop or commune**: no public dataset; only national totals in the press. A transparency request would be needed.
- **heladas.minagri.gob.cl frost history**: could not be opened.
- **Older Crop Reports**: the current Frutas de Chile site holds PDFs only from February 2025; earlier seasons exist (a news title mentions the 2018-19 season) but their location was not found.
- **Weekly exports by region before 2025** in table form: only press summaries were seen.
- **INIA Agromet API**: agromet.inia.cl shows a blank page; no documented API for agrometeorologia.cl.
- **CR2MET after 2021**: no newer version found, so it cannot check 2022.
- **Argentina**: no piedmont pruning dates and no dated piedmont bloom observations; SENASA open export data only for January to July 2020; no blueberry insurance or claims data; no Corrientes or Buenos Aires blueberry declarations; SMN archive depth unknown (documentation pages returned 403); the APAMA website now hosts unrelated content (sub-pass).
- **Uruguay**: no blueberry area by department, no open blueberry export series, no blueberry-specific frost or hail declaration, no verified website for producer associations (sub-pass).
