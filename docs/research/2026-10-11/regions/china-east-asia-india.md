# China, Japan, South Korea and India: blueberry information sources (2026-10-11)

Slug: `china-east-asia-india`. Companion file: [`china-east-asia-india.sources.json`](china-east-asia-india.sources.json) (76 sources, 22 extraction jobs). All checks were made on 2026-10-11. Statements marked [inference] are my reasoning, not source text.

## 1. Scope and summary

I covered mainland China (Yunnan, Shandong, Liaoning, Guizhou, Jilin, Sichuan), Japan, South Korea and India. I searched in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi and English. Japan, Korea and India were researched by three helper agents under the same rules; I checked their records against the schema and merged them. The JSON holds 26 China, 19 Japan, 16 Korea and 15 India sources. 60 were opened, 13 were reachable only in part, and 3 were seen only in search results.

Five most valuable findings:

1. **Qingdao bloom dates now exist.** A 2025 paper by the Huangdao District Meteorological Bureau and a Qingdao grower gives phenophase windows for open-field and cold-shed blueberries: open-field first bloom 21 to 25 April, full bloom 26 April to 12 May; cold-shed first bloom 21 to 25 March. It also reports that orchard minimum temperatures ran 2.7 C (2019) and 2.9 C (2020) below the Huangdao national station ([Rao et al. 2025, 北方农业学报](https://journal30.magtechjournal.com/bfnyxb/CN/abstract/abstract9395.shtml), Table 1 and section 2.1.1).
2. **A national calendar and province table for China.** Li Yadong's 2024 industry report gives area and output for the top eight provinces, the protected-cultivation share, and ripening windows by region. It states that evergreen cultivars in Yunnan flower in August to September and ripen in November ([full text republished on sqtv.net](http://sqtv.net/news/bencandy.php?fid=103&id=253081)).
3. **Blueberry-specific weather services exist in Liaoning and Guizhou.** The Liaoning Meteorological Bureau runs a strawberry and blueberry service centre in Dandong and reports dated damage, such as hail on 21 June 2021 ([ln.cma.gov.cn](http://ln.cma.gov.cn/xwzx/qxxw/202107/t20210713_3523136.html)). Guizhou published weekly blueberry agrometeorological bulletins with station tables in 2020 ([gznw.com](https://www.gznw.com/eportal/ui?currentPage=1&pageId=597466&moduleId=ce9c7674e29443278394ad59eb9b3191&articleKey=719393&columnId=620088)).
4. **Japan and Korea have official statistics with blueberry rows.** MAFF publishes blueberry area, harvest and shipments for every prefecture from 2003 to 2023 under a CC BY 4.0 compatible licence ([MAFF](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/tokei/kouhyou/tokusan_kazyu/index.html)). The Tokyo Central Wholesale Market gives monthly blueberry arrivals by origin prefecture back to 2002 ([shijou.metro.tokyo.lg.jp](https://www.shijou.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/torihiki/geppo)). Korea has census blueberry area by province and county, split into open field and protected, and blueberry crop insurance since 2024 ([KOSIS](https://kosis.kr/statHtml/statHtml.do?orgId=101&tblId=DT_1AG25411&conn_path=I2), [data.go.kr](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15126188/fileData.do)).
5. **Station data: NOAA is the practical route, and it has a gap.** CMA station data needs real-name registration and gives only 7 days of free hourly data ([data.cma.cn](https://data.cma.cn/data/cdcdetail/dataCode/A.0012.0001_api.html)). NOAA GSOD and ISD have Mengzi (569850), Dandong (544970) and Qingdao (548570) back to the 1940s and 1950s, but every file I checked, including Miami and Tokyo, stops on 24 August 2025 and the 2026 files return 404 ([isd-history.csv](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/noaa/isd-history.csv)). [inference] This looks like an NCEI processing pause that affects every region, not only China.

## 2. Production context in brief

**China.** China is the largest producer. Li Yadong's survey puts 2024 area at 1.4382 million mu (about 95,900 ha) and output at about 780,000 t; the top provinces by area are Guizhou, Yunnan, Liaoning, Sichuan, Anhui, Shandong, Jilin and Jiangsu, and Yunnan leads output with 182,900 t ([sqtv.net republication](http://sqtv.net/news/bencandy.php?fid=103&id=253081); [chinanews 2025-02-27](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2025/02-27/10375305.shtml)). Xinhua, via SCIO, reports 1.58 million mu and 810,000 t for 2025 ([SCIO 2026-04-24](http://english.scio.gov.cn/m/chinavoices/2026-04/24/content_118461086.html)). Protected cultivation covered 366,000 mu in 2024, and substrate culture made up 79.36% of it; in Yunnan all protected area is substrate ([sqtv.net](http://sqtv.net/news/bencandy.php?fid=103&id=253081)). The same report gives ripening windows: Yunnan and the Panzhihua dry-hot valleys January to June and mid-August to December; Jiaodong (Shandong) open field early June to mid-July; Liaodong open field July; Changbai Mountains mid-July to mid-August; northern solar greenhouses February to May.

- *Yunnan (Mengzi, Honghe, Chengjiang):* greenhouse evergreen SHB on substrate. The Mengzi government gives a picking season from November to June ([hhmz.gov.cn](https://www.hhmz.gov.cn/info/5911/358731.htm)) and targets 30,000 mu of substrate blueberry and 72,000 t by 2026 ([action plan](https://www.hhmz.gov.cn/info/5261/299321.htm)).
- *Shandong (Qingdao Huangdao, Rizhao):* heated greenhouse, cold shed and open field. Trade press puts the Qingdao season from March to June with a core in late May to June and open-field fruit from late June ([FreshPlaza China](https://www.freshplaza.cn/article/9534408/mu-qian-zheng-shi-qing-dao-lan-mei-shang-shi-de-you-shi-shi-duan/)).
- *Liaoning (Dandong, Zhuanghe):* forced solar greenhouses and open field with buried canes. Kuandian greenhouse fruit runs from mid-March to the end of April and open-field fruit from May to the end of August ([Liaoning NDRC 2021](https://fgw.ln.gov.cn/fgw/xxgk/jgjc/fxyc/C1F45498AC6341BFB490CA33119897E4/index.shtml)).
- *Guizhou (Majiang):* mostly rabbiteye, largely for processing; Majiang had 87,600 mu and 39,900 t in 2024 ([China Daily 2025-08-15](https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202508/15/WS689f1af5a3104ba1353fd149.html)).
- *Jilin (Baishan, Jingyu):* cold-region open field; Baishan reports 53,000 mu ([Jilin investment page](https://www.jl.gov.cn/szfzt/tzcj/zdxm/ncpjg/202604/t20260422_3625908.html)).

**Japan.** Small, scattered, mostly open-field highbush and rabbiteye. In 2023 Japan had 983.4 ha and 2,263.8 t; Tokyo, Nagano, Gunma, Ibaraki and Chiba lead ([e-Stat xlsx](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?statInfId=000040426410&fileKind=0)). Domestic fruit fills June to August; imports from Mexico, the USA, Chile and Canada cover October to May ([e-Stat trade CSV](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?statInfId=000040424869&fileKind=1)). Iwate trials give mean full bloom between 8 and 17 May and harvest peaks between 9 and 29 July for six NHB cultivars, 2013 to 2023 ([Iwate ARC report 1169](https://www.pref.iwate.jp/agri/_res/projects/project_agri/_page_/002/011/532/repo_1169.pdf)).

**South Korea.** The RDA calendar lists three systems: open field (budbreak mid-March, bloom early April, ripening early June, peak shipment mid to late June), unheated house and heated house ([Nongsaro](https://nongsaro.go.kr/portal/ps/psb/psbl/workScheduleDtl.ps?menuId=PS00087&cntntsNo=258549&sKidofcomdtySeCode=210002&totalSearchYn=Y)). The 2024 insurance target area for blueberry was 435.9 ha in Gyeonggi, Chungbuk, Jeonbuk, Jeonnam and Gyeongbuk ([data.go.kr 15042054](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15042054/fileData.do)). Press figures of 3,393 ha in 2024 could not be traced to an official table.

**India.** Small and young. IBO describes low-chill SHB, a main season from mid-February to early June, and protected systems for pest pressure (IBO record `in-monsoon`). Dated trial data come from Himachal Pradesh: at CSKHPKV Palampur, rabbiteye harvest started on 21 May (Alapaha) and 27 May (Austin) in the 2015-16 trial ([IJAgS](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/view/79138)), and a review gives flowering in February to March and ripening in April to May ([Indian Horticulture](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IndHort/article/view/111443)). No official area or production statistics exist.

| `cn-sd-qingdao-huangdao` | deciduous NHB/SHB, mixed protection | Deciduous 100%, chill clock, 2,757 chill h | Mar to Jul | Jun to Jul (recall 0.4) | none published |
| `cn-ln-dandong` | deciduous NHB/SHB, mixed protection | Deciduous 100%, chill clock, 3,635 chill h | Feb to Aug | Jul to Aug (recall 0.29) | none published |

The low recall at Qingdao and Dandong comes from greenhouse months (February to May) that a chill-clock model of open-field plants cannot produce. The registry's gap list says bloom months were not found for any of the three sites, Mengzi pruning timing is undocumented, Li Yadong's reviews were not read, and there are no volume statistics for Mengzi peak months.

**IBO regional evidence** (`dist/evidence/ibo-regional-v1.json`): ten records in scope. `cn-yunnan-calendar` (December start, February to mid-April peak, later November start), `cn-yunnan-induction` (shade-cloth flowering induction, zero-chill primocane systems), `cn-yunnan-2024-dry-heat` (dry spring and April-May heat 2024), `cn-shandong-greenhouse` (back-wall greenhouses March to late May, about 10% of area), `cn-northeast-harvest` (season ends in Liaoning and Jilin in August; frosts), `cn-liaoning-monsoon-undated` (monsoon rain doubled or tripled in the Liaoning harvest window, year not stated), `cn-guizhou-rabbiteye`, `cn-sichuan-quality`, `cn-land-policy` and `in-monsoon`. There are no benchmark sites and no IBO records for Japan or Korea.

**What this research adds:** bloom windows for Qingdao (dated) and for Liaodong and Yunnan (month level); open-field versus protected splits; seven dated damage events (one, the Yunnan frost of late November 2025, still needs an official source); station IDs; four official statistics series outside China; and the first market series for China, Japan and India.

## 7. Access, licensing and language risks

- **China, permissions.** NMC forbids download and reuse of its content without written authorisation. chinanews, sqtv.net and the Liaoning NDRC site forbid reproduction without permission. p5w owns its original content. These suit an internal, link-only corpus with paraphrased claims. Public display of full text needs permission first.
- **China, access.** CMA station data need real-name registration; whether a mainland phone or ID is required could not be confirmed. Customs statistics sit behind a bot wall (HTTP 412). MARA price platforms and the KOSIS viewer are JavaScript apps. CNKI full text is paid. The Qingdao paper's PDF has no usable text layer and needs OCR.
- **China, data quality.** Industry area and output figures disagree between sources and years. The 2024 report numbers are survey estimates. Government targets are often reported as if achieved. Keep targets, surveys and statistics as separate record types.
- **NOAA gap.** GSOD and ISD files end on 24 August 2025 for every station checked. Any bias correction must stop at that date or use national services.
- **Japan.** MAFF and JMA data are reusable with attribution under PDL 1.0. The Tokyo market statistics are copyrighted; publish derived indices only, unless permission is granted. Japan Blueberry Association material is members-only. e-Stat API access needs an app ID (free registration).
- **Korea.** KMA data are KOGL Type 1 (attribution). RDA data are KOGL Type 4 (no change, no commercial use) and Nongsaro is Type 2 (no commercial use). Garak history needs an application. API keys need free registration.
- **India.** IMD station data are paid and foreign institutes pay full charges. TradeStat changes HS codes from April 2026. Most evidence is in English; a few state news items are in Hindi.
- **Language.** Chinese, Japanese and Korean sources need careful translation of stage terms (for example 初花期 first bloom, 盛花期 full bloom, 満開期 full bloom, 개화기 bloom period) and of dekad expressions (上旬, 中旬, 下旬; 초순, 중순, 하순). Record the original term in each claim.
