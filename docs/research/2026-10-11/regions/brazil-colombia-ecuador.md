# Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador: blueberry information sources for extraction and regional evidence

Slug: `brazil-colombia-ecuador`. Checked on 2026-10-11. Companion file: [`brazil-colombia-ecuador.sources.json`](brazil-colombia-ecuador.sources.json) (93 sources, 23 jobs).

## 1. Scope and summary

I searched in Portuguese, Spanish and English and covered Brazil (Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina including the Planalto Norte and Papanduva, Paraná, São Paulo and the Minas Gerais highlands), Colombia (Cundinamarca, Boyacá and Antioquia) and Ecuador (Sierra and coast). Every URL in the JSON was fetched: 71 opened, 21 opened-limited, 1 search-only. Two sub-agents did the first pass for Colombia and Ecuador. I spot-checked their main claims; for example, the Colombian EVA API returned 289 arándano rows for 2019-2025, as they reported.

Five most valuable findings:

1. **Brazil has an open, municipal, day-dated disaster file back to 1991.** The [Atlas Digital de Desastres](https://atlasdigital.mdr.gov.br/paginas/downloads.xhtml) CSV (version 2026-08-06, 1991-2025) has `Data_Evento`, `Cod_Cobrade`, `Cod_IBGE_Mun` and a private agricultural loss column, `PEPR_Agricultura (R$)`. Hail rows (code 13213) and frost rows (code 13322) appear for São Joaquim, Fraiburgo and other blueberry municipalities. The live S2iD system blocks requests from outside Brazil ([s2id.mi.gov.br](https://s2id.mi.gov.br/) returned HTTP 403: "somente a partir de enderecos IP localizados no Brasil"). The Atlas file is the workable route.
2. **Brazil's crop insurance subsidy data is per policy, geolocated and coded by cause.** [SISSER/PSR open data](https://dados.agricultura.gov.br/dataset/sisser3) (CC BY, 2006-2025) has municipality, coordinates, crop, `VALOR_INDENIZAÇÃO` and `EVENTO_PREPONDERANTE` (GEADA, GRANIZO, SECA, CHUVA EXCESSIVA and others). In 12 MB of samples I found apple, grape, plum and peach policies but no mirtilo rows. Temperate fruit is the realistic proxy for flowering-freeze and hail years.
3. **Santa Catarina has the only open monthly harvest distribution for blueberry, and the Planalto Norte had none in 2014/15.** [Epagri/Cepa Fruticultura em números 2014/15](https://docweb.epagri.sc.gov.br/website_cepa/Fruticultura/Doc_271_Fruticultura_em_Numeros_2014_15.pdf), Figure 23, gives monthly harvest shares by Epagri region. Campos de Lages: December 37%, January 18%, February 41%, March 4%. UGT 4 Canoinhas, the region that includes Papanduva, lists 0 blueberry municipalities. Epagri also publishes station chill hours at or below 7.2 °C, the model's threshold, against a 2008-2026 mean ([Monitoramento do frio 065/2026](https://ciram.epagri.sc.gov.br/ciram_arquivos/site/monitora_frio/it_065_outubro_2026.pdf): Lages 440 h in 2026 against a mean of 422).
4. **Colombia has open municipal area and monthly export series.** The [EVA 2019-2025 dataset](https://www.datos.gov.co/Agricultura-y-Desarrollo-Rural/Evaluaciones-Agropecuarias-Municipales-EVA-2019-20/uejq-wxrr) (CC BY-SA) has 289 arándano rows, about 907 ha planted in 2025. [MADR monthly trade](https://www.datos.gov.co/resource/rfip-pkv5.json) gives HS 0810400000 exports by origin department; 2025 exports peak in December (165.5 t) and January (131.6 t). [IDEAM hourly temperature](https://www.datos.gov.co/Ambiente-y-Desarrollo-Sostenible/Temperatura-Ambiente-del-Aire/sbwg-7ju4) covers Guasca, Sopó, Tausa and Paipa. [UNGRD records](https://www.datos.gov.co/resource/2343-nuqp.json) give dated municipal HELADA and GRANIZADA events. I found no public national list of ICA export-registered blueberry farms.
5. **Ecuador has station truth and one dated phenology record, but no loss data.** INAMHI's viewer has an open, undocumented JSON API (720 hourly Tmin values for Tomalón-Tabacundo, M1094) and [yearbooks for 1994-2014](https://servicios.inamhi.gob.ec/anuarios-metereologicos/). A [UNL thesis](https://dspace.unl.edu.ec/server/api/core/bitstreams/ebdb8500-0fc7-4242-8e00-6e00a82029b7/content) records Biloxi bloom from 24 Feb to 17 Mar 2022 and harvest from 14 Jul to 15 Sep 2022 at 2,150 m. [INEC ESPAC 2024 microdata](https://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/encuesta-de-superficie-y-produccion-agropecuaria-continua-bbd/) has 7 ARÁNDANO rows. I found no primary source for IBO's "95% of volume in the Andes".

Two smaller finds also matter. A [Paraná trial at IAPAR Cerro Azul](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbf/a/B4wvqmqZsXB9pVfWByCt9zj/?lang=en) (659 m, 2012/13-2014/15, CC BY 4.0) gives dated bloom and harvest for a new low-chill site. A [Vacaria union report](https://www.strvacaria.com.br/geada-tras-prejuizos-aos-produtores/) dates a frost on 12-14 September 2015, when blueberry flowers were open, with an estimated 30% blueberry loss.

## 2. Production context in brief

**Brazil.** The crop is small, scattered and growing.
- Brazil had about 400 ha, mainly in Rio Grande do Sul and Santa Catarina, with smaller areas in São Paulo, Minas Gerais and Paraná. Most cultivars were rabbiteye ([Medeiros et al. 2018](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbf/a/B4wvqmqZsXB9pVfWByCt9zj/?lang=en), citing Cantuarias-Avilés et al. 2014).
- Brazil does not appear in FAO blueberry data. Production is spreading to Bahia, Pernambuco and the Cerrado (DF). Biloxi and Emerald dominate the cultivar mix. MAPA has 38 registered cultivars. New orchards use anti-bird and anti-hail nets ([Embrapa authors, Anuário HF 2026](https://www.infoteca.cnptia.embrapa.br/infoteca/bitstream/doc/1185598/1/Mirtilo.pdf)).
- Rio Grande do Sul had 74 ha and 358 t in 2023 (Emater/RS figure in the [Radiografia da Agropecuária Gaúcha 2024](https://www.agricultura.rs.gov.br/upload/arquivos/202408/26113434-rag-2024-22-08-24-final-capa-atualizada.pdf)). Commercial planting began at Vacaria in 1990 (Embrapa, already cited in the benchmark).
- Santa Catarina in 2014/15 had 23 producers, 22.9 ha and 51.9 t, in the Oeste, Campos de Lages and Curitibanos regions. Harvest ran from November to March depending on region ([Epagri/Cepa 2014/15](https://docweb.epagri.sc.gov.br/website_cepa/Fruticultura/Doc_271_Fruticultura_em_Numeros_2014_15.pdf)).
- Family farms in the Planalto Norte took up blueberry from about 2017, with Campo Alegre named; Papanduva is not named ([Revista da Fruta, Feb 2025](https://www.revistadafruta.com.br/noticias-do-pomar/o-avanco-do-mirtilo-em-santa-catarina-e-a-nova-rota-das-frutas-vermelhas,451320.jhtml)).
- In Paraná, floral initiation usually starts in June and can run to September or October. Bloom lasts 7 to 14 days and fruit ripens 2 to 3 months after bloom. Trials at Lapa, Pinhais and Cerro Azul favour rabbiteye. The cost model uses a December to January harvest ([SENAR-PR 2025](https://www.sistemafaep.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PR.0398-A-cultura-do-mirtilo_web.pdf)).
- At Cerro Azul, cultivars flowered between July and September and were harvested between November and January ([Medeiros et al. 2018](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbf/a/B4wvqmqZsXB9pVfWByCt9zj/?lang=en)).
- In São Paulo, UF low-chill cultivars arrived in 2010. Growers aim to avoid the December to February price collapse ([Cantuarias-Avilés et al. 2014](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbf/a/cG47Fsqm583VwcmNtb8KQWv/abstract/?lang=pt)).

**Colombia.**
- In 2025, EVA records 437 ha and 3,264 t in Boyacá and 420 ha and 2,711 t in Cundinamarca. The largest municipalities are Sotaquirá and Tausa ([EVA uejq-wxrr](https://www.datos.gov.co/Agricultura-y-Desarrollo-Rural/Evaluaciones-Agropecuarias-Municipales-EVA-2019-20/uejq-wxrr)).
- The industry body says about 1,000 ha and 12,000 t, with 95% sold at home ([El Colombiano](https://www.elcolombiano.com/negocios/arandanos-colombia-2025-produccion-exportaciones-expansion-BE36459080)). EVA therefore probably under-counts production [inference].
- Plantings sit at 2,600-3,000 m. There are three production peaks, the largest in November-December, timed by pruning. Biloxi predominates ([IBO web, Sep 2025](https://www.internationalblueberry.org/2025/09/24/colombias-growing-blueberry-industry-record-imports-and-expansion-of-high-altitude-crops/)).
- The largest grower uses coir pots, protects against frost with sprinklers and does not prune seasonally ([Redagrícola](https://redagricola.com/elite-blu-y-las-claves-del-manejo-del-arandano-en-colombia/)).
- Radiative frosts in the altiplano come mainly in January ([DANE SIPSA frost note](https://www.dane.gov.co/index.php/estadisticas-por-tema/agropecuario/sistema-de-informacion-de-precios-sipsa/impacto-de-las-heladas-en-los-precios-mayoristas-y-abastecimiento)).

**Ecuador.**
- About 400 ha were planted by 2025, about 300 ha of them in production ([El Universo](https://www.eluniverso.com/noticias/economia/arandanos-ecuador-exportaciones-nuevos-mercados-cultivo-en-desarrollo-nota/)).
- Production is mostly in the Sierra at 2,000-3,000 m, under macrotunnels or greenhouses, often hydroponic ([Portal Berries](https://portalberries.com/como-compites-si-el-arandano-peruano-entra-al-pais-a-3-dolares-el-kilo/)). 95% of plantations are semi-hydroponic on coir ([Forbes Ecuador](https://www.forbes.com.ec/negocios/el-arandano-ecuatoriano-superfood-futuro-n96658)).
- Pruning is used to supply all 52 weeks of the year. Hortifrut has about 50 ha at Zapotillo (Loja) and shipped to the USA on 23 Dec 2022 ([Agrocalidad](https://www.agrocalidad.gob.ec/arandanos-ecuatorianos-ya-conquistan-el-mercado-de-estados-unidos/)). The first certified export came from Cayambe in November 2021 ([Agrocalidad](https://www.agrocalidad.gob.ec/primera-exportacion-de-arandanos-a-paises-bajos/)).

## 3. What the app already has for this scope

- **Benchmark sites** (`pipelines/weather/benchmark_sites.json`; results in `docs/weather/benchmark/benchmark.json`). There are five sites.
  - Guasca (Colombia, evergreen managed cycle): harvest recall 0.33.
  - Vacaria (RS): model harvest Oct-Dec against a published Nov-Apr. Vacaria bloom months rest on snippets only.
  - Pelotas (RS): model harvest Oct-Dec against a published Dec-Jan, so the model is too early.
  - Lages (SC): model bloom Jul-Sep against a published Sep-Oct.
  - Piracicaba (SP, evergreen): model bloom Apr-Jul against a published October.
  - Papanduva is the supervisor's reference site and has no bloom or harvest source. The registry's `gaps` section lists the Cerro Azul dataset as a candidate low-chill site.
- **IBO regional evidence** (`dist/evidence/ibo-regional-v1.json`). CO-HIGHLANDS has two records: `co-three-peaks` (IBO 2023 and 2025 calendars) and `co-tunnels` (hail and rain push larger farms under tunnels). EC has one record, `ec-year-round` (95% of volume in the Andes; pruning trials near Guayaquil). A search of the file for Brazil found no records, so the Regional evidence tab is empty for Brazil.
- **Earlier research.** `04_validation_data_and_protocol.md` already lists the INMET annual zips and Epagri/Ciram (176 stations, registration). `07_weather_inputs_and_global_scaling.md` reports the station pilot: grid minus station Tmin of +0.90 °C at Major Vieira and +1.33 °C at Rio Negrinho, and all sub-zero days missed. `GLOBAL_PHENOLOGY_REVIEW.md` summarises Pelotas, Piracicaba, Cerro Azul and Guasca, and states that no Papanduva calendar was established.
- **Known gaps this research targets.** No observed, dated, cultivar-specific stage data; no loss data; the Pelotas harvest is modelled too early; Papanduva has no calendar; Colombia has only one primary trial (2014); Ecuador rests on one IBO record.

### 4.0 Top 10 sources, described from an actual sample

1. **Atlas Digital de Desastres** (`br-atlas-digital-desastres`). I read the CSV header and samples with byte-range requests; the file is 86 MB. Fields: `Protocolo_S2iD; Nome_Municipio; Sigla_UF; Data_Registro; Data_Evento; Cod_Cobrade; descricao_tipologia; Cod_IBGE_Mun; Status; DH_*; DM_*; PEPL_*; PEPR_Agricultura (R$); PEPR_total_privado`. Example rows: `SC-F-4216503-13213-20250419; São Joaquim; Granizo; event 19/04/2025` and `SC-F-4205506-13322-20210730; Fraiburgo; event 30/07/2021`. The second row is labelled "Onda de Frio" although COBRADE 13322 is frost, so filter by code, not label.
2. **SISSER/PSR policies** (`br-psr-sisser-apolices`). From the header and a 6 MB range of the 2016-2024 CSV (124,353 lines): `NM_MUNICIPIO_PROPRIEDADE; SG_UF_PROPRIEDADE; LATITUDE...; NM_CULTURA_GLOBAL; NR_AREA_TOTAL; NR_PRODUTIVIDADE_SEGURADA; DT_INICIO_VIGENCIA; DT_FIM_VIGENCIA; ANO_APOLICE; CD_GEOCMU; VALOR_INDENIZAÇÃO; EVENTO_PREPONDERANTE`. Event counts in the sample: SECA 24,470; GRANIZO 7,410; GEADA 5,596; CHUVA EXCESSIVA 1,620. Example: Bom Jardim da Serra (SC), Maçã, indemnity 97,262, GRANIZO. No mirtilo rows were found.
3. **Epagri/Cepa Fruticultura em números 2014/15** (`br-epagri-cepa-fruticultura-2014-15`). Tabela 33 Mirtilo has columns UGT, region, municipality, producers, total ha, harvested ha, t, kg/ha, R$/kg and gross value; São Joaquim had 11 producers, 5.8 ha and 9.2 t. Figure 23 gives the monthly harvest shares by UGT quoted in section 1.
4. **Epagri Monitoramento do frio** (`br-ciram-monitoramento-frio`). Issue 065/2026, Tabela 1: `Localidade | ID – Estação | 2026 | 2025 | 2024 | MÉDIA`, with values such as São Joaquim 1049, 811/942/715 h against a mean of 770, and Caçador 1056, 398/497/307 h against a mean of 423.
5. **Emater/RS Informativo Conjuntural** (`br-emater-rs-informativo-conjuntural`). Issue No. 1935 (2 Sep 2026), section Frutícolas: "Na região administrativa da Emater/RS-Ascar de Soledade, a cultura de mirtilo se encontra em início de floração." The colophon dates the series from June 1989 and allows reproduction with citation.
6. **INMET stations** (`br-inmet-estacoes-automaticas`). One call to `apitempo.inmet.gov.br/estacoes/T` returned 671 stations with `CD_ESTACAO, DC_NOME, VL_ALTITUDE, DT_INICIO_OPERACAO, CD_SITUACAO`. Examples: A880 Vacaria at 969.89 m since 2008; A864 Major Vieira at 799.58 m since 2009; A815 São Joaquim at 1400.06 m, status "Pane". The download page lists annual zips from 2000.zip to 2026.zip.
7. **Colombia EVA 2019-2025** (`co-eva-upra-2019-2025`). SoQL query on `cultivo` = Arándano. Fields: `departamento, municipio, a_o, periodo, rea_sembrada, rea_cosechada, producci_n, rendimiento, estado_f_sico_del_cultivo`. My own grouped query returned row counts by year of 25, 30, 33, 37, 45, 54 and 65 (2019-2025), and planted area rising from 265.2 ha to 906.7 ha.
8. **Colombia MADR monthly trade** (`co-madr-trade-monthly`). Fields: `partida, descripci_n_departamento, pais, a_o, mes, ton_netas_expo, valor_miles_fob_dol_expo`. 2025 monthly export tonnes: January 131.6, February 36.9, March 26.8, April 2.1, May 13.2, June 2.5, July 9.6, August 1.8, September 40.7, October 56.5, November 87.2, December 165.5.
9. **IDEAM observations and station catalogue** (`co-ideam-hydromet-obs`, `co-ideam-station-catalog`). Fields: `codigoestacion, fechaobservacion, valorobservado, descripcionsensor, latitud, longitud`. Guasca [21205700] at 2,750 m has hourly 2 m temperature from 2024-10-29. Tunguavita (Paipa) runs from 2003.
10. **INAMHI API and yearbooks** (`ec-inamhi-api-hourly`, `ec-inamhi-anuarios`). The API returned the station list (1,881 stations, 220 transmitting) and 720 hourly "TEMPERATURA DEL AIRE MIN 1 HORAS" values for M1094 (lowest 8.17 °C). The 2011 yearbook gives M1094's monthly absolute minimum with its day (January: 5.4 °C on day 18).

Also sampled: UNGRD frost rows (2023-01-30, Siachoque, HELADA, 400 ha), DANE SIPSA weekly arándano prices (Corabastos mean 46,717 COP/kg, 3-9 Oct 2026), INEC ESPAC ARÁNDANO rows, and the Cerro Azul full text.

## 7. Access, licensing and language risks

- **Geoblocking and bot walls.**
  - S2iD returns HTTP 403 outside Brazil.
  - The Emater/RS archive page timed out, although the PDFs on state hosts work.
  - datosabiertos.gob.ec returned HTTP 403.
  - The FINAGRO sites were unreachable.
  - The Conab Prohort portal timed out.
  - A Brazil- or Colombia-based collaborator, or a run from the lab server, may be needed. This needs a human decision.
- **Registration.** Epagri/Ciram hourly data needs a free account. History beyond 24 months, IDR-Paraná station data and IDEAM DHIME bulk data are available on request by e-mail.
- **Personal data.** The PSR files hold insured names and partial CPF numbers. Some ICA lists name farms. Publish aggregates or vereda-level points only.
- **Licences.**
  - CC BY: SISSER, Rev. Bras. Frutic. articles, INEC ESPAC and UNGRD 2025.
  - CC BY-SA 4.0: Colombian datos.gov.co EVA, trade and IDEAM data. Share-alike applies to derived tables.
  - ODbL: the BCB SICOR OData API.
  - Emater/RS allows reproduction with citation.
  - SENAR-PR forbids reproduction without permission.
  - No terms were found for the Atlas, INMET, Epagri/Ciram, INAMHI, DANE SIPSA, Comex Stat or the press sources. Use citation-first records and short quotes.
- **Data-quality traps.**
  - Atlas frost rows are labelled "Onda de Frio".
  - PSR gives no loss date.
  - EVA may carry forward values and under-counts production compared with industry figures.
  - UNGRD 2025 rows are duplicated in pairs.
  - The INAMHI API is undocumented and returns 30 days by default.
  - One web-search summary falsely said the Epagri Síntese 2025 had a mirtilo table; the table belonged to the garlic chapter.
- **Language.** All primary sources are in Portuguese or Spanish. Extraction prompts and reviewers need both languages. Stage terms differ ("início de floração", "plena floração", "inicio de cosecha"), so map them to the IBO stage vocabulary.
